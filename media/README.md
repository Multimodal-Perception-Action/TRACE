# Media

`method.pdf` and `method.png` reproduce the project's manuscript method figure. The figure illustrates the research pipeline; a robot controller is not included in this inference release. The separate conversational editor is described in the [editing guide](../docs/REFERENCES_AND_EDITING.md).

## Generated examples

The three MP4 files below were generated by the released standing-conditioned workflow, including smoothing and heel/toe four-point contact refinement. All use 20 FPS and 960 x 720 H.264 video. They show final SPS output, not raw denoiser output.

| File | Input | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `walk.mp4` | A person walks forward at a steady pace. | 5.00 s |
| `wave.mp4` | A person stands and waves the right hand. | 5.05 s |
| `jump.mp4` | Perform a jumping jack. | 2.55 s |

The PNG files are previews of the corresponding clips. The wave is restrained and the jumping jack has mild asymmetry. These examples are not claims of benchmark performance, physical balance, or safe robot execution. Only selected media is distributed; local request, environment, and model-path reports are excluded.

## Real-robot demo

`real_robot_demo.mp4` contains the physical-robot sections of the TRACE paper demo, concatenated without re-encoding. It is a silent, 1920 x 1080, 30 FPS H.264 video with 716 frames (23.87 seconds). `real_robot_demo.jpg` is a preview from its dance section.

| Start | Content | Source section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.00 s | Jumping-jack editing, before and after | `04_editing.mp4` |
| 6.40 s | Two in-the-wild instructions | `05_wild.mp4` |
| 13.50 s | Three dance examples | `06_test.mp4` |

These sections come from `TRACE_demo_OMG_GT_duration.mp4`; the method-comparison renderings and opening title are omitted. The jump is played once, and the original playback speed is retained. Within each multi-clip panel, shorter recordings hold their final frame.
