# Media

`method.pdf` and `method.png` reproduce the project's manuscript method figure. The figure illustrates the research pipeline; a robot controller and the separate conversational editor are not included in this inference release.

The three MP4 files were generated by the released standing-conditioned workflow, including smoothing and heel/toe four-point contact refinement. All use 20 FPS and 960 x 720 H.264 video. They show final SPS output, not raw denoiser output.

| File | Input | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `walk.mp4` | A person walks forward at a steady pace. | 5.00 s |
| `wave.mp4` | A person stands and waves the right hand. | 5.05 s |
| `jump.mp4` | Perform a jumping jack. | 2.55 s |

The PNG files are previews of the corresponding clips. The wave is restrained and the jumping jack has mild asymmetry. These examples are not claims of benchmark performance, physical balance, or safe robot execution. Only selected media is distributed; local request, environment, and model-path reports are excluded.
